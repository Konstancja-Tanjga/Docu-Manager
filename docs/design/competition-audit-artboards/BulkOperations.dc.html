ZIP export and bulk operations

The selection model is specified once because getting it wrong once gets it wrong everywhere: the misunderstanding that exports 50 of 460 documents also deletes 50 of 460.

1
Row checkboxes and a master checkbox
The master selects what is currently loaded, and says so.
BLK-1
2
“Select all filtered results” is a separate action
Two acts, two labels: fifty on this page, or all 460 matching these filters. Conflating them is the single most common cause of an incomplete export.
BLK-2BDL-3
3
The selection limit is stated inline, before the attempt
Next to the action, naming the limit and the current selection. A limit discovered by rejection wastes the selection the user just built.
BLK-5
4
Bulk delete is logical and requires a reason
The document leaves the working views, stays recoverable, and stays subject to retention and legal hold. The reason goes into the audit entry.
BLK-7PRM-18
5
Metadata export offers a format choice in the dialog
Not a global setting — the recipient differs per export.
BLK-8
6
Results land in two places
A toast says it finished; a bulk-operations panel holds what did not, and why. The toast is gone by the time anyone reads the failures.
BLK-11BLK-12

Every one of these reports per item, never per batch — 460 documents where 12 fail leaves 448 done and names the 12 with a reason each (BLK-10).
