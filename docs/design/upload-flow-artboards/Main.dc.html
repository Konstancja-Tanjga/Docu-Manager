Why Continue is disabled

Nothing is selected, and UPL-8 says neither option may be assumed. A preselected default here is the whole failure mode: the person clicks the primary button without reading, and either the repository gains a duplicate or a document quietly gains a version of something else. The button turns on when a choice is made — and for the restore path, when the comment is written.
